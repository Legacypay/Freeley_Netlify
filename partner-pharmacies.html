Partner Pharmacies

Our Commitment to Transparency & Quality

Freeley is committed to providing the highest quality healthcare experience. As a telehealth platform, we partner with fully licensed, US-based 503A compounding pharmacies to fulfill prescribed medications. All partner pharmacies are held to rigorous quality and safety standards.

Together with our partner Strive Pharmacy, we are proud to serve patients nationwide across all 50 U.S. states.
