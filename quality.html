How Prescriptions Are Issued

Freeley does not practice medicine, employ physicians, or issue prescriptions. Instead, we connect patients with independently licensed, board-certified physicians through our clinical partner MD Integrations. Here is how the process works:

1

Patient Completes Health Assessment

You complete a comprehensive medical questionnaire covering your health history, current medications, allergies, and treatment goals.

2

Board-Certified Physician Review

A board-certified physician licensed in your state independently reviews your case. The physician may approve, request additional information, or determine that treatment is not appropriate for you.

3

Prescription & Pharmacy Fulfillment

If medically appropriate, the physician issues a prescription that is fulfilled by our PCAB-accredited partner pharmacy, Strive Pharmacy. Medications are compounded to your specific prescription and shipped directly to your door.

Important: Prescriptions are only issued when medically appropriate based on a legitimate patient-prescriber relationship. If our physician determines that treatment is not right for you, you will be notified and will not be charged for the medical consultation. Follow-up care is available through your prescribing physician via the Freeley Hub portal.
