What is Sermorelin?

Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide that stimulates the pituitary gland to release more growth hormone. Originally developed to treat growth hormone deficiency, Sermorelin has gained popularity for its anti-aging benefits. The Role of NAD+ in Health

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a crucial coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It plays a vital role in energy metabolism and cellular repair. As we age, NAD+ levels decline, leading to reduced energy production and increased susceptibility to age-related diseases. By replenishing NAD+ levels, you can potentially slow down the aging process and improve overall health.
