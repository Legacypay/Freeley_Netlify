### What Are Peptides?

Peptides are naturally occurring in the body and are essential in orchestrating a variety of physiological processes. They serve as signaling molecules, influencing how our bodies perform tasks such as hormone production, immune function, and cellular repair.

- **Better Cognitive Function:** Some peptides are known to support brain health, improving memory and cognitive abilities.
