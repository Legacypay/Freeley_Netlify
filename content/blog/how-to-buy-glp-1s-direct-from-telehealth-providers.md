## Understanding GLP-1s and Their Benefits

GLP-1s, or glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists, are a class of medications primarily used in managing type 2 diabetes. They mimic the action of the natural hormone GLP-1, enhancing insulin secretion and reducing blood sugar levels. Additionally, GLP-1s have gained attention for their role in weight management, making them a dual-purpose treatment for many patients.
