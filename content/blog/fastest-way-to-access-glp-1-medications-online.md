## Understanding GLP-1 Medications

GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1) medications have become increasingly popular for their role in managing type 2 diabetes and aiding in weight loss. These medications mimic the body's natural GLP-1 hormone, which helps regulate blood sugar levels and appetite. With the rise of telehealth, accessing these medications has become faster and more convenient.

## Benefits of GLP-1 Medications

**Efficient Blood Sugar Control:** GLP-1 medications enhance insulin secretion, reducing blood sugar spikes after meals.

**Weight Management:** By slowing gastric emptying and increasing satiety, these drugs help in weight loss.

**Cardiovascular Health:** Some studies suggest that GLP-1 medications may improve heart health by reducing cardiovascular risk factors.
