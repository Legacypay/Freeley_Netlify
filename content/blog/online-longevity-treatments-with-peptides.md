## The Science Behind Peptides

### What Are Peptides?

Peptides are smaller than proteins and can easily penetrate cells, making them ideal candidates for therapeutic interventions. They function as signaling molecules within the body, influencing numerous biological processes, including metabolism, immune responses, and cellular repair.

### How Do Peptides Promote Longevity?

Research indicates that certain peptides can mimic the body's natural growth factors, leading to enhanced cellular regeneration, improved immune function, and reduced inflammation. By targeting these key areas, peptides may slow down the aging process and extend healthy living.
