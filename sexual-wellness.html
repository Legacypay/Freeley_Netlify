How Sublingual Troches Work

Unlike traditional oral medications that must pass through the digestive system, sublingual troches dissolve under the tongue for direct absorption into the bloodstream. This can mean faster onset and more consistent results.

Our compounded 3-in-1 formula combines Tadalafil (for long-lasting effect), Vardenafil (for rapid onset), and Apomorphine (which works on brain pathways to enhance arousal). When prescribed by a licensed provider, this combination approach addresses ED from multiple angles.
