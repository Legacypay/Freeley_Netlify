Understanding GLP-1 Treatment

GLP-1, or glucagon-like peptide-1, is a type of medication often used to improve blood sugar control in people with type 2 diabetes. It mimics the functions of the incretin hormone, which helps to stimulate insulin secretion, slow gastric emptying, and reduce appetite. GLP-1 treatments have also gained popularity for their role in weight management.
