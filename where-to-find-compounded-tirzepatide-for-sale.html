What is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide is a dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist. This novel medication works by enhancing insulin secretion and reducing appetite, making it highly effective for blood sugar control and weight loss.
