Understanding GLP-1 Medication

GLP-1 medications, or glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists, are a class of drugs primarily used to treat type 2 diabetes and assist with weight management. They work by mimicking the GLP-1 hormone, which increases insulin secretion, inhibits glucagon release, and slows gastric emptying. This combination helps to regulate blood sugar levels and reduce appetite.
