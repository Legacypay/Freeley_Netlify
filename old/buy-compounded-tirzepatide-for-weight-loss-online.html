Understanding Tirzepatide and Its Role in Weight Loss

Tirzepatide is a breakthrough medication originally designed to manage type 2 diabetes. Recently, it has garnered attention for its potential weight loss benefits. This dual-action drug works by mimicking two hormones, GLP-1 and GIP, that regulate blood sugar and appetite. As a result, tirzepatide helps individuals achieve significant weight reduction alongside improved metabolic health.

Potential Risks and Considerations

While compounded tirzepatide offers benefits, there are inherent risks:
