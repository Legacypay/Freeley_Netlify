FAQ

Your safety, ensured

Are the medications FDA-regulated?

Freeley partners with licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies that operate under FDA-regulated frameworks. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved drug products; they are prepared in licensed pharmacies under a valid prescription from your physician.

Will I be matched with a real doctor?

Yes. Every patient is reviewed by a board-certified physician licensed in your state. Your provider signs off on the treatment plan before any prescription is sent to the pharmacy.

What if I have side effects?

Your provider checks in throughout your program and can adjust your dose or pause your prescription at any time. You can message your care team directly through your patient portal.

Is my health information secure?

Yes. Freeley is HIPAA-compliant. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is never sold to third parties for advertising.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts and no membership fees. You can pause or cancel your treatment plan at any time from your account.

Do I need insurance?

No insurance is required. Your monthly cost is transparent and covers your consultation, prescription and shipping.
