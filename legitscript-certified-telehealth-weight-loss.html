Understanding LegitScript Certification

What is LegitScript Certification?

LegitScript is an organization that provides certification for healthcare-related websites and telehealth platforms. This certification ensures that these platforms comply with legal requirements and standards, promoting transparency and consumer protection. LegitScript's rigorous evaluation process includes:

  • Verification of Licensing: Ensuring that telehealth providers are appropriately licensed to offer medical services.
  • Adherence to Regulations: Compliance with regulations specific to telehealth and online services.
  • Quality Assurance: Evaluation of the quality and safety of the services offered.
