A Deeper Look Inside GLP-1

What is GLP-1?

GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) is a natural hormone your body produces in response to food. It plays a key role in regulating appetite, blood sugar levels, and how your body processes energy.

Compounded GLP-1 medications like Semaglutide and Tirzepatide mimic this hormone, helping reduce appetite and food cravings, slow gastric emptying so you feel full longer, and support your body's natural metabolic processes.

When prescribed by a licensed provider and combined with healthy lifestyle changes, GLP-1 therapies can be a powerful tool in your weight management journey.
